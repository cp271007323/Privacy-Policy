# KindBounds Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KindBounds (“we,” “us” or “our”) handles information in the KindBounds app on iPhone and iPad and in support correspondence. KindBounds is the app operator.

The app’s parental controls operate on your device. No KindBounds account is required, and the app does not upload your rules or usage reports.

## Information on your device

The app stores your selected apps and categories, entered website domains, limits, schedules and their names, extra time, restrictions, language and reminder preferences, custom category names, and execution status needed to apply rules. Apple’s selection interfaces use protected identifiers where supplied by the system.

Rules and execution settings are shared locally between the app and its own Screen Time extensions. This version has no remote pairing, remote approval or cloud rule synchronization.

## Usage reports

Apple’s protected Device Activity report extension processes system-provided usage for selected apps and categories, which may include associated websites, and displays reports on your device. The main app does not read or export the raw usage dataset. Reports are not uploaded by this app. Apple controls the underlying usage history and required authorization.

## Passcode and recovery code

Apple’s Keychain stores salted verification data for the parent passcode and recovery code, plus failed-attempt and lockout information. The parent passcode is not stored as plain text.

A new recovery code remains readable in the Keychain until you confirm saving it. The app then removes the displayable copy and keeps verification data. Recovery is verified locally, without a network service. We cannot retrieve or send you these credentials remotely.

## Authorization and permissions

Apple handles Screen Time authorization, including required parent or guardian approval for a child and individual authorization for self-management. The app’s parent passcode is separate. If Apple uses Face ID or Touch ID, the app receives the authorization result, not facial images, fingerprints or biometric templates.

Local reminders are optional and may show schedule names on the lock screen according to your notification settings. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings. Turning off reminders does not turn off system restrictions.

The current features do not access your camera, microphone, location or photo library.

## Use and sharing of information

Local information is used to apply your rules, display system reports, verify management access, remember preferences and schedule reminders. We do not sell, rent or use this information for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking. This version has no advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

Our extensions and Apple’s services receive information needed for these features. Apple authorization may require an Apple account and network access. Apple distribution, backups and system diagnostics are governed by your settings and the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Communications with us

If you email us, we receive your address and message. We use them to respond to support and privacy requests. Email providers handle delivery.

Do not include your parent passcode, recovery code or unnecessary identifying information about a child.

## Retention and deletion

Local settings remain for your configured features until edited or removed. You can review or revoke permissions in iOS Settings, subject to Apple’s parent or guardian controls.

Uninstalling normally removes app-container data, but may leave Keychain items, data retained by another app, system usage history or backups. Apple’s device and backup controls govern those records.

We have no server copy of local rules, reports or management credentials to delete remotely. Support emails remain only as needed for inquiries, privacy requests and applicable legal obligations.

## Children and family use

Parents or guardians should authorize and review a child’s setup. Core features do not require a child’s name, email address or app account. Activity information is used locally for configured controls and reports, without advertising.

## Security and privacy choices

We use Apple’s protected storage for rule documents, Keychain for credentials and parent verification for sensitive changes. Keep the device, passcode and recovery code secure.

If applicable law gives you rights to access, correct or delete information we hold, contact us below. This can cover support correspondence. Information held only on your device must be managed through the app and Apple’s device controls.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and its date when privacy practices change, with additional notice where applicable law requires.
